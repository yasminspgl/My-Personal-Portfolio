MyShop — Mobile Shopping App

A cross-platform retail app built in Xamarin.Forms (C#/.NET): a full storefront with home, category pages (clothing, beauty, furniture, home décor), product listings and a shopping basket. University project, BMAN11000.

  • Xamarin.Forms
  • C# / .NET
  • University
How I'd scale this

Right now the product list is written straight into the app. To make it ready for real use, I'd split it into three parts:

  • a front end for the screens people see,
  • an API in the middle,
  • and a database that stores the products and orders.

Then the catalogue and basket come from the database instead of being fixed in the code, so items can be added or updated without changing the app itself.

2025 Dissertation

Design, Deployment, and Sustainability

A Grounded Theory Analysis of Energy Demand in Open- and Closed-Source Large Language Models

My final-year dissertation at Alliance Manchester Business School (ITMB). Using constructivist grounded theory, it examines how the design and deployment of open-source models (LLaMA, DeepSeek) versus closed-source models (ChatGPT, Claude) shape their energy demand and environmental impact — and what transparency, standardisation, and governance can do about it.

2025 Article

Transformation by Design: Rebuilding Texas Instruments, 1985–1996

Strategic management essay · University of Manchester

Using Teece's dynamic-capabilities framework, this essay argues that Texas Instruments' 1985–1996 turnaround was driven by CEO Jerry Junkins' shift to a decentralised structure — countering cognitive inertia, enabling contextual ambidexterity, and reshaping the firm's identity — as its share price climbed from roughly $1 to $9.

2024 Article

Blind Spots at the Top: Bounded Rationality & Dominant Logic at Microsoft

Strategic management essay · University of Manchester

A dynamic-capabilities analysis of why Microsoft's sensing capability faltered in the 2000s and early 2010s: the bounded rationality of its top team and an entrenched "Windows is god" dominant logic filtered out mobile opportunities. It recommends a larger, more diverse top team and structural differentiation to reframe threats as opportunities.
